# School selection

signage-apps fork: `assets/static/data/quotes.json` is a hand-picked subset of
Screenly's verified set (wording and attribution were already fact-checked
upstream, see AUDIT.md; nothing was reworded here). Kept: encouraging, plain
quotes that suit students of any age. Left out: cynical or adult jokes (e.g.
the "life is like a sewer" line), romance and marriage, religion, violence,
business and money, current or polarising political figures, and lines too
abstract for children.

To restore the full set, take quotes.json from the Screenly-Labs/quotes repo.
